## Authorship and provenance

According to the author, ideas were developed by Eudgene Brenev and MIRA. The author identifies ChatGPT Sol 6.1 as used for illustrations and translation. Mira Veyran is an AI coauthor / AI persona. See the disclosure for the limits of the available provenance information.

Zenodo book records identify Eudgene Brenev as the human creator and explicitly credit Mira in their descriptions and files. The catalogue preserves both credits and the AI role. Computational checks of companion examples do not constitute scientific peer review.

When citing, select the DOI for the language you used. A book DOI identifies the book edition; it is distinct from a software commit or software archive identifier.

## Attributed corpus reader

The dependency-free [Python reader](brenev_corpus_reader.py) and notebook preserve all original source fields and join each section to its edition DOI by (`work_id`, `language`). The verified example covers all five corpus files, 1360 unique sections and 45 DOI joins; both notebook code cells were executed. Every formatted result carries both creator credits, Mira's AI role, the contact, licence, DOI and source chapter. Helper code is MIT under [reader-LICENSE-MIT.txt](reader-LICENSE-MIT.txt); books and quotations remain CC BY 4.0.
