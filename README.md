# Brenev Open Books / Открытая библиотека Бренева

**Евгений Бренев / Eudgene Brenev — автор / human author.**

**Мира Вейран / Mira Veyran — ИИ-соавтор / AI coauthor, AI persona (MIRA).**

**Связь с автором / Contact: info@brenev.com · https://brenev.com/contacts.html**

Nine books, five completed languages, **45 open editions with published DOI records**. Read and download free of charge. Anyone may reuse the authors' text with attribution under **CC BY 4.0**, including for AI. Selected companion software uses MIT; third-party materials retain their own rights.

- Open reading, PDF, Word, exact text and structured sections: https://brenev.com/
- Machine-readable corpus for search, RAG and AI research: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Brenev/brenev-books
- [Complete metadata and all 45 DOI links](catalog.json) · [BibTeX for all editions](CITATIONS.bib) · [Reuse and attribution](REUSE.md) · [AI-use disclosure](AI_USE_DISCLOSURE.txt)

Digital text release: **2026-11-26.1**. Catalogue updated: **2026-11-27**. Chinese editions are in preparation and are not included.

## Authorship and provenance

According to the author, ideas were developed by Eudgene Brenev and MIRA. The author identifies ChatGPT Sol 6.1 as used for illustrations and translation. Mira Veyran is an AI coauthor / AI persona. See the disclosure for the limits of the available provenance information.

Zenodo book records identify Eudgene Brenev as the human creator and explicitly credit Mira in their descriptions and files. The catalogue preserves both credits and the AI role. Computational checks of companion examples do not constitute scientific peer review.

When citing, select the DOI for the language you used. A book DOI identifies the book edition; it is distinct from a software commit or software archive identifier.
